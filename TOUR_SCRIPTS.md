## HOW THIS FILE IS STRUCTURED

Each stop has:
- **DISPLAY TITLE** — shown in the footer title bar (all 4 locales)
- **DISPLAY LINES** — bullet points shown in the footer (all 4 locales)
- **HOST line** — the presenter's spoken/shown dialogue (all 4 locales)
- **PUNDIT line** — the analyst's reply (all 4 locales)

Locales: `EN` = English (UK) · `US` = English (US) · `SCO` = Scottish · `NO` = Norwegian
